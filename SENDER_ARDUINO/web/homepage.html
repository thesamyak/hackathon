THE PROBLEM

As AI moves from generating answers to taking real-world actions, a simple question becomes critical: should an AI be trusted to directly control hardware? Direct access exposes physical systems to unpredictable, erroneous, or malicious AI behaviors.

[02]

THE TRUST LAYER

ActuLock explores this problem by placing an independent trust layer between an AI agent and a physical system. Every AI-generated command is checked against predefined authorization, safety, and operational constraints before it reaches the hardware.

[03]

THE PROTOTYPE

Our prototype uses an Arduino Nano and LED-based actuation to demonstrate the filtration process. It visually showcases how mathematically and logically valid commands are permitted, while unsafe or unauthorized commands are firmly rejected at the hardware boundary.

[04]

THE VISION

The larger vision is to create a scalable safety and security layer for AI-controlled cyber-physical systems. This includes critical infrastructure such as robotics, industrial automation pipelines, autonomous transport systems, and smart grids.
